How this works in practice

The production cycle of one kit is four months. With an agreed annual delivery plan the partner receives the product from stock on a pre-arranged day, so the effective lead time is one day for transport. Production planning stays on our side.

The partition is supplied ready for trimming and installation; trimming, fitting and finishing are carried out by the partner.

  • Two electrically operated folding seats
  • Central folding box, electrically operated
  • Electrochromic glass with electric raise-and-lower mechanism
  • Samsung 43-inch television with electric lift
